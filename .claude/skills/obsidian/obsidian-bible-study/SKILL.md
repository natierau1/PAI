# Bible Study Note Skill

Create structured Bible study notes with passage analysis, practical application, cross-references, and integration with daily reflections in ~/Documents/Obsidian/Personal/

## What This Skill Does

Generates comprehensive Bible study notes that:
- Provide structured passage analysis
- Include observation, interpretation, and application
- Track cross-references and related passages
- Support character studies
- Enable topical studies
- Link to daily reflections and life applications
- Connect to personal growth and spiritual disciplines
- Integrate with weekly/monthly reviews

## Vault Location

All paths reference: `~/Documents/Obsidian/Personal/`

Suggested locations for Bible study notes:
- **Scripture Studies**: `Atlas Dots/Scripture/` or `Planes/Spiritual/Scripture/`
- **Character Studies**: `Atlas Dots/People/Bible Characters/`
- **Topical Studies**: `Atlas Maps/Spiritual/[Topic]/`
- **Daily Devotions**: Link from `Diary/[YYYY]/[MM-Month]/` daily notes

## Best Practices

- Study Bible with open heart for God to speak
- Focus on understanding before application
- Look for context (historical, literary, theological)
- Let Scripture interpret Scripture (cross-reference)
- Ask: What does this teach about God? About me?
- Move from observation → interpretation → application
- Document questions to research further
- Track how passages apply to real life
- Link studies together for comprehensive understanding
- Review and meditate on key passages regularly
- Share insights with others
- Pray before, during, and after study

## When to Use

- When needing guidance for a decision
- When studying a Bible book systematically
- When researching a biblical topic
- When preparing a sermon or lesson
- During small group study
- When processing a spiritual question
- For personal spiritual growth
- When memorizing Scripture
